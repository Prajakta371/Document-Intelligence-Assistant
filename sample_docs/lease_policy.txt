Harbour Lease and Finance - Client Handbook (sample document)

Section 1: Equipment Lease Terms
Standard equipment leases run for 24, 36 or 48 months. Clients may end a lease early after the first 12 months by paying an early termination fee equal to three monthly payments. At the end of the lease the client can return the equipment, extend the lease month to month, or buy the equipment at its residual value, which is normally 10 percent of the original price.

Section 2: Payments and Late Fees
Payments are due on the first business day of each month. A 7 day grace period applies. After the grace period a late fee of 35 dollars is charged, and a second late fee is charged for every further 14 days the payment remains overdue. Clients who expect to miss a payment should contact us before the due date.

Section 3: Insurance
The client must keep the leased equipment insured for its full replacement value for the whole lease term. A certificate of insurance must be provided within 14 days of the lease start date.

Section 4: Mortgage Pre-Approval
A mortgage pre-approval is valid for 90 days. To apply, clients must provide two recent payslips, three months of bank statements and a current photo ID. Pre-approval is not a guarantee of finance.

Section 5: Financial Hardship and Complaints
Clients facing financial hardship should contact us within 5 business days of a missed payment so we can discuss a repayment plan. Complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days and we aim to resolve them within 21 days.
